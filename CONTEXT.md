# Plasma Context

Plasma is a one-page student prototype for the Pi.Ca.Ci.U 2026 Area 1 CULTURE brief. The service preserves and shares personal memory through a digital touchpoint while keeping a light connection to physical objects and their emotional value.

## Language

**Plasma**:
The service name for the digital touchpoint that collects, organizes, preserves, and shares personal memories.

**Oggetto fisico**:
A tangible personal item whose emotional value and story can be preserved digitally.

**Memoria affettiva**:
The personal or relational meaning attached to an object, place, event, file, or experience.

**Atom**:
The bubble-like cover and topic container for a modular bento box of files, media, and memory fragments.
_Avoid_: Bubble as a public-facing synonym.

**Atom Bento Box**:
The composition space where a user assembles files, media, text, and context before creating an **Atom**.

**Freeze**:
A time-capsule feature that schedules the future unlocking or sharing of an **Atom** with selected people.

## Relationships

- Plasma should keep the current Figma structure and content direction while adding a light, explicit connection to **Oggetto fisico** and **Memoria affettiva**.
- An **Atom** contains multiple files or memory fragments around one specific topic.
- An **Atom Bento Box** produces one **Atom** once the selected contents and context are assembled.
- **Freeze** applies to an **Atom** and controls when selected people can access it.
- Plasma should remain open to a generic mix of memory scenarios rather than using one dominant narrative example.
- The prototype should not become a different service concept unless the team explicitly changes this decision.

## Tone Of Voice

- Keep the bilingual direction from the Figma: Italian explanatory copy can coexist with English product labels, CTAs, and section titles.

## Example Dialogue

> **Dev:** "Should the website be rebuilt around physical objects from scratch?"
> **Domain expert:** "No — keep the current Figma direction, but make the relationship with objects and emotional memory clearer where it helps the brief."

## Flagged Ambiguities

- The Figma currently communicates a broad digital memory app more strongly than an object-centered cultural service. Resolved: use a light reframing, not a full conceptual rewrite.
- The term **Bubble** overlaps with the user definition of **Atom**. Resolved: avoid **Bubble** in public-facing copy and use **Atom** as the canonical term.
- The bilingual Figma tone is intentional, but individual English labels can still be refined if they weaken clarity.
